# <img src="../../img/ic/i_seed_daikon.png" width="32" height="32" alt=""> Getting started

<img src="../../img/title.webp" width="720" alt="">

The four picture buttons on the title screen: sprout = new game, book = continue, gear = settings, moon and futon = quit.

A new game starts with a short opening, then you design your look and wake up to your first morning. On day one, things to try appear as picture cards and footprints lead the way.

There is no text in the game. Talk, hints and menus are made of pictures, emote bubbles, gestures and numbers.
