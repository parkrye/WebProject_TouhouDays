# <img src="../../img/ic/i_workbench.png" width="32" height="32" alt=""> Processing, crafting, research

<img src="../../img/library.webp" width="720" alt="">

- Things are made in stages: raw material → processed → parts → finished goods. For example, cut bamboo, dry it, split it into strips and weave a basket.
- Build workstations at the workbench and place them. Long jobs run on their own while you do something else.
- Research (book button) starts when you’re in the same place as the resident who mentors that field, and finishes the next morning. It unlocks recipes, bag slots, tool upgrades, house extensions and more.
- In cooking, the flavors of the ingredients carry over into the dish.
