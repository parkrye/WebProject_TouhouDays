# <img src="../../img/ic/i_chochin.png" width="32" height="32" alt=""> Festivals

<img src="../../img/natsumatsuri.webp" width="720" alt="">

On set days, festivals are held in the village, at the shrine and elsewhere. Eat at the stalls, play minigames at the game booths and watch the night-sky shows.

Hatsumode, Setsubun, cherry-blossom viewing, Tanabata, the summer festival, Obon, moon viewing, autumn-leaf viewing, the underground hot-spring festival and the snow festival.
