# <img src="../../img/ic/e_happy.png" width="32" height="32" alt=""> People

<img src="../../img/village.webp" width="720" alt="">

- The people of Gensokyo live their own days, with schedules, moods and memories. Talk to them with the action key or a tap, and give them gifts.
- As you grow closer (hearts), special scenes play, and you can ask them to come along with you or trade appointments and invitations. Meeting places are marked on the map.
- Sit together on chairs and benches, or have a drink by the road and drink-lovers may wander over. You can even throw a banquet with a newspaper ad.
- Hold a game board and talk to someone to challenge them: shogi, chess, gomoku, reversi and flicking stones. Learn the rules in Board-game lessons in the menu.
