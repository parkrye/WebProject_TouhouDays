# <img src="../../img/ic/e_q.png" width="32" height="32" alt=""> Good to know

- The game saves automatically when the day changes. You can also save any time from the menu.
- When an appointment or destination is marked on the map, footprints show you the way.
- Not sure what a button does? Hover it (PC) or long-press it (mobile).
- Adjust the sound in Settings on the title screen or in the menu.
